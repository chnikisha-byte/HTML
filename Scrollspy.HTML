Section 1

Codingal

Codingal is an online platform where students learn coding and programming in a simple and interesting way. It provides live classes with trained teachers who guide students step by step. Students can learn from home and attend one-to-one sessions to clear their doubts. Codingal offers different courses like Scratch for beginners, Python, Java, web development, game development, and app development. Students get a chance to work on real projects and build their own games, websites, and apps. Coding helps improve logical thinking, problem-solving skills, creativity, and confidence. The classes are interactive, and teachers give regular feedback to help students improve. Codingal also provides certificates and conducts competitions to encourage students. It prepares students for the future by developing important technical skills in a fun and engaging way.
