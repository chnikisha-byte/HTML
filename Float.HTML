Flowers are the reproductive, often colorful, structures of angiosperm plants, designed to produce seeds through pollination by insects, birds, or wind. Comprising parts like petals, sepals, stamens, and pistils, they play a crucial role in plant reproduction. There are roughly 250,000 to 400,000 species, ranging from roses and sunflowers to orchids, serving as key food sources for pollinators and holding significant ecological and cultural value.
